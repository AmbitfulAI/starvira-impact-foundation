# Editing content and publishing stories

You do not need to know how to code. Sign in at `/admin` (see CMS-SETUP.md).

## Publish a story

1. **Stories and blog > New Story.**
2. Fill in the title, date, author, category, summary and body. Use the toolbar for headings, bold, lists, links and images.
3. Add a featured image (landscape, at least 1600 pixels wide) and describe it in **Image description** for screen reader users.
4. To link the story to a programme page, choose it under **Related programmes**.
5. Untick **Sample article** for real stories.
6. Save. Move the card to **In review**, then **Ready**, then press **Publish**.

**Schedule a story:** set a future publication date. It appears on the first rebuild after that date (daily, if the scheduled rebuild is set up).
**Hide a story:** tick **Draft**.

## Update programmes

**Programmes** lets you edit each programme's summary, challenge, activities, outcomes, indicators and partners. When a programme launches, change **Status** from Planned to Pilot or Active.

## Update contact, bank, social and impact details

**Site settings > Contact, bank, social and impact figures.**

- Replace every value in square brackets, such as `[Account Number]`. Bracketed values are hidden on the public website, so the related line or section only appears once real information is entered.
- **Social media:** paste a profile link only when the account is active. Empty links show as "coming soon".
- **Online giving:** paste a Paystack or Flutterwave payment page link to switch on the "Give securely online" button.
- **Impact figures:** enter a value only when it has been verified under the MEL framework. Empty values show "Data pending".
- **Reports:** upload annual report PDFs to list them on the Impact page.

## Update the founder page

**Site settings > Founder page.** Replace the biography and story placeholders, review the founder's message, and change **Message status** to approved.

## Photographs and consent

- Use only photographs for which you hold informed written consent. For children, a parent or guardian must consent in writing.
- Never publish a child's full name with their photograph.
- Show people as capable and hopeful, never as helpless.
- Large photos are fine: keep them under about 2 MB each by exporting at 1600 to 2000 pixels wide.

## Writing style

Warm, clear and specific. British English spelling. No em dashes: use commas, colons or full stops. Only publish numbers you can verify.
