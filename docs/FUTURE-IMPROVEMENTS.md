# Suggested future improvements

1. **Real photography.** Commission a consent-based photo shoot at the first programme activities and replace illustrations on the home and programme pages.
2. **Online giving.** Open a Paystack or Flutterwave account in the Foundation's registered name and add the payment page link in Site settings. Later, add automated receipts through the provider's webhooks.
3. **Newsletter platform.** Move newsletter sign-ups from email notifications into a mailing tool such as Brevo or Mailchimp, with double opt-in.
4. **Impact dashboard.** Once data is flowing, connect the impact cards to a simple verified spreadsheet export.
5. **Yoruba translation.** Add Yoruba versions of key pages for community audiences.
6. **Analytics.** Enable a cookie-free analytics service (Plausible is pre-wired in Site settings) to learn what visitors use.
7. **Volunteer management.** If volunteer numbers grow, connect the volunteer form to a shared tracker or a volunteer management tool.
8. **Accessibility audit.** Arrange an independent review once real content is in place.
