## Overview

Community Empowerment Drives put residents at the centre. The Foundation listens first, agrees priorities with the community, and then brings together volunteers, partners and resources to act.

## Dignity in giving

When the Foundation distributes support, it does so respectfully. Recipients are not required to be photographed, distributions are organised to avoid long waits and public embarrassment, and selection criteria are explained openly.
