## Overview

The Back to School Project is the Foundation's starting point in education. It focuses on a simple but decisive moment: the beginning of the school term. By removing practical barriers at that moment, and staying in touch afterwards, the project helps children stay in school long enough for learning to take hold.

## How it works

Support is guided by need rather than favouritism. Schools, community leaders and families help identify children, and the Foundation confirms each case before support is given. Every child supported is followed up during the term, because a school bag alone does not guarantee a place in the classroom.

## Safeguarding

All activities involving children follow the Foundation's safeguarding policy. Volunteers are briefed before they take part, children are never photographed without written consent from a parent or guardian, and personal information is stored securely.
