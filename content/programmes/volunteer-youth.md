## Overview

Volunteers are the heartbeat of a community foundation. This programme makes volunteering well organised, safe and rewarding, so that people who give their time are supported, trained and appreciated.

## Growing leaders

Young volunteers do not only help deliver programmes. They are invited to propose ideas, lead small projects and grow into leaders in their own communities.
