## Overview

Healthy Living Outreach brings practical, trustworthy health information into communities, and builds bridges to the people and places that provide care. The emphasis is on prevention, early action and dignity.

## Our role, clearly stated

Starvira Impact Foundation is not a medical provider. The Foundation facilitates health education, awareness and access. Any screening, clinical advice or treatment offered at outreach events is delivered only by qualified, licensed professionals working within their scope of practice and with appropriate arrangements in place.

## Wellbeing as part of health

Health includes the mind as well as the body. Sessions on emotional wellbeing aim to open conversation, encourage people to seek support early, and point them towards appropriate professional help.
