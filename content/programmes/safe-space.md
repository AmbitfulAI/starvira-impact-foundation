## Overview

The Safe Space programme is built around dignity, inclusion and protection. It gives girls and women a place to learn, grow and lead, and it involves families and communities in creating environments where every girl can thrive.

## Safeguarding first

This programme follows strict safeguarding standards. Facilitators are vetted and trained, sessions are supervised, and information shared by participants is treated with confidentiality. The Foundation does not provide counselling, legal or clinical services itself. Where specialist help is needed, participants are referred to qualified providers.
