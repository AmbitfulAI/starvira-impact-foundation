## Overview

Women Rise exists to help women move from survival towards stability and growth. The programme combines practical skills with confidence, community and access to opportunity.

## Approach

Learning is practical and respectful of the knowledge women already bring. Sessions are scheduled with participants' household and work responsibilities in mind, and peer circles continue after formal training ends so that progress can be sustained.
