## Overview

Skills and Livelihood Training turns potential into practical ability. The programme focuses on skills that are in demand locally, taught by people who practise them, and connected to real opportunities for work.

## Inclusion

Training is designed to be inclusive. The Foundation works to make venues, schedules and materials accessible, and actively encourages applications from women and persons with disabilities.
