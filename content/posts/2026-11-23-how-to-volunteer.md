Volunteers will be at the heart of everything we do. Whether you have an afternoon a month or professional skills to share, here are five ways to get involved.

1. **Help on distribution days.** Assemble school packs and support community drives.
2. **Mentor.** Share your experience with a young person or a woman starting a business.
3. **Facilitate.** If you are a trainer, health professional or teacher, help lead sessions within your area of expertise.
4. **Tell our story.** Writers, designers and photographers can help us communicate with care and consent.
5. **Become a youth ambassador.** Young people aged 16 and above can help us reach their peers, with parental consent where under 18.

## What to expect

Every volunteer receives an induction, including our safeguarding expectations. We will match you to activities that suit your availability and skills, and we will recognise your contribution.

Ready to begin? Register your interest on our Get Involved page.
