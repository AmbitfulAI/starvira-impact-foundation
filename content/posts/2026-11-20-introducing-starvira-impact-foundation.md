Starvira Impact Foundation begins with a simple belief: every person carries potential worth recognising, protecting and nurturing.

Our name brings together two ideas. A **star** speaks of light, hope and guidance. **Vira** speaks to us of faith, courage and the determination to overcome difficulty. Together they describe the work we want to do: lighting paths, and lifting lives.

## Where we are starting

Our work begins in Akure and Akungba Akoko, in Ondo State. Starting close to home means we can listen carefully, build trust and learn what works before we grow.

## What we will focus on

We have organised our work around four connected areas:

- **Education and Youth Development**, so that children start school and stay there, and young people gain the skills and confidence to lead.
- **Health and Well-being**, through community health education and links to qualified care.
- **Economic Empowerment and Livelihoods**, so that women and young people can build stable incomes.
- **Equality, Protection and Community Inclusion**, so that every girl and woman has the space and support to thrive.

## How we will work

We will work alongside communities rather than for them. We will measure what we do honestly, report it openly, and treat every person we serve with dignity.

If our purpose speaks to you, we would be glad to hear from you. You can volunteer, propose a partnership, or simply stay in touch through our newsletter.
