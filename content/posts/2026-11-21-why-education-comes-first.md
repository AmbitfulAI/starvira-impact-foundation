When a new school term begins, most families are juggling several costs at once: books, writing materials, uniforms and levies. For a household already under pressure, the first week of term can be the hardest week of the year.

## Small gaps can grow

A child who misses the first days of term can fall behind quickly. Once a pupil feels lost in class, it becomes easier to stay at home, and harder to return. What started as a short-term money problem can become a long-term learning problem.

## Timing is part of the solution

Our **Back to School Project** is designed around that moment. Practical support delivered before term begins, followed by regular check-ins with pupils, parents and teachers, can help a child walk into class prepared and stay there.

> A school bag alone does not keep a child in school. Encouragement and follow-up do.

## What we will measure

We will track whether supported pupils start term on time, how regularly they attend, and whether they are still enrolled a year later. We will share what we learn, including what does not work as well as we hoped.
