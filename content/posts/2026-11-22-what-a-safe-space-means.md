A safe space is more than a room. It is a promise: that the people who gather there will be respected, listened to and protected.

## Three things we hold on to

**Safety comes first.** Facilitators are vetted and trained. Sessions are supervised. What participants share is treated with care and confidentiality.

**Confidence is built, not given.** Through leadership activities, conversations about rights and practical life skills, participants grow in self-belief at their own pace.

**Communities matter.** Girls thrive when parents, leaders, boys and men support equal opportunity. That is why sensitisation with the wider community is part of the programme.

## Knowing our limits

We are clear about our role. Where a participant needs counselling, legal advice or medical care, we connect her to qualified providers rather than attempting to replace them.
